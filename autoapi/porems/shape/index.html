class porems.shape.Hourglass(inp)¶

Bases: Shape

Cylindrical shape whose radius varies as a cosine along the axis, narrowing to a minimum at the mid-point.

Required inputs:

  • central - Central axis unit vector

  • centroid - Centroid of the block

  • length - Total length along the axis

  • diameter_outer - Diameter at both ends (widest)

  • diameter_inner - Diameter at the mid-point (narrowest)

The radius profile is

\[r(z) = r_i + (r_o - r_i)\,\frac{1 + \cos\!\left(\frac{2\pi z}{length}\right)}{2}\]

with outer radius \(r_o\), inner radius \(r_i\) and length \(length\).

Parameters:

inp (dictionary) – Dictionary of necessary inputs
